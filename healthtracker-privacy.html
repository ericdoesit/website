Health Tracker — Privacy Policy

Effective July 11, 2026

The short version: your data stays on your device. Health Tracker has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads, and no third parties. We can't see your data — nobody can but you.

What Health Tracker stores

Health Tracker keeps everything you log — weight, body composition, nutrition, workouts, supplements, medications, reminders, and settings — in local storage on your iPhone. None of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else. There is no sign-up, no account, and no cloud database.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, Health Tracker reads weight, body fat, lean mass, waist circumference, nutrition, steps, activity, and workouts from Apple Health, and can save workouts and body measurements back to Apple Health so your data stays in sync.

  • Health data is used only to power the app's tracking and coaching features on your device.
  • Health data is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and is never shared with or disclosed to any third party.
  • You can revoke Health Tracker's access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps.

Backups and exports

You can export your data as a backup file or report. Exports happen only when you initiate them, and the file goes wherever you choose to save it (for example, a folder in the Files app). You control where that file lives and who sees it.

Notifications

Reminders and check-ins use local notifications scheduled on your device. They don't involve any server.

Analytics and tracking

Health Tracker contains no analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising frameworks, and no trackers. If you install beta builds through TestFlight, Apple may share basic beta statistics (like install counts and crash logs) with the developer under Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

Health Tracker is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the effective date revised before those changes take effect.
